  Question 1. Let K=R,C and identify the Lie algebra of an embedded Lie subgroup of GL(n,K) with a Lie subalgebra of Mat(n×n,K). Prove that

•

The Lie bracket on the Lie subalgebra of Mat(n×n,K) is the standard commutator of matrices.

•

The Lie algebras of classical groups are the following Lie subalgebras of Mat(n×n,K).

- The Lie algebras of the special linear groups SL(n,K) readsl(n,K)={M∈Mat(n×n,K):tr(M)=0}.- The Lie algebras of the orthogonal groups O(n) reado(n)={M∈Mat(n×n,R):M+MT=0}.- The Lie algebras of the unitary groups U(n) readu(n)={M∈Mat(n×n,C):M+M†=0}.- The Lie algebras of the special orthogonal groups SO(n) readso(n)=o(n).- The Lie algebras of the special unitary groups SU(n) readsu(n)={M∈Mat(n×n,C):M+M†=0,tr(M)=0}.
