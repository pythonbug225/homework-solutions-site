124 秋图论初步期末试卷 (吴河辉)

试题共八道题, 其中前四题每题 10 分, 后面四题每题 20 分, 选三题作答, 并在上面方框中划去不做的题目.

1.

Which pair of graphs below are isomorphic? ​

2.

How many isomorphism classes of 1204-vertex trees with diameter 3? ​

3.

What is the minimum size of a maximal matching in the cycle C2024​? ​

4.

What is the number of faces in a 100-vertex plane graph with average degree 3.14? ​

5.

Let G be an r-connected graph of even order having no K1,r+1​ as an induced subgraph. Prove that G has a 1-factor.

6.

Prove that every subgraph of Kn,n​ having at least n(n−1)+2 edges has cycles of all even lengths from 4 through 2n.

7.

Prove that every triangle-free graph with at most 2t2+3t−2​ vertices is t-colorable.

8.

Let G be a graph with δ(G)=3 and dˉ(G)<310​. Prove that G has a 3-vertex whose neighbors have degree-sum at most 10.

223 秋图论初步期末试卷 (吴河辉)

试题在六道题里选做五题, 并在答题纸上划去不做的题目.

1.

For k>2, prove that if the vertices of a k-regular graph partitions into sets A and B that each induces a tree, then ∣A∣=∣B∣.

2.

Prove that every nontrivial tree has a maximum matching that covers every non-leaf vertex plus at least one leaf.

3.

Prove or disprove: if G is a 2-connected graph that is not a cycle, then it contains a cycle C such that G−V(C) is connected.

4.

Place n vertices around a circle. Let Gn​ be the 4-regular graph obtained by making each vertex adjacent to the two nearest vertices in each direction. For n≥5, prove that Gn​ is the union of two Hamiltonian cycles.

5.

A parity edge-coloring of a graph G is an edge-coloring such that on every path, some color appears an odd number of times, let p(G) denote the minimum number of colors in a parity edge-coloring of G. Show that p(K5​)=7.

6.

Prove that every plane triangulation with minimum degree 5 has two 3-faces sharing an edge such that the non-shared vertices have degree-sum at most 11. (Hint: Use Vertex discharging, and each 5-vertex get 1/5 charge from each neighbor first.)

322 秋图论初步期末试卷 (吴河辉)

试题在六道题里选做五题, 并在答题纸上标明不做的题目.

1.

Let E(A,V−A) be an edge cut of a graph G with even size, and v is an odd vertex of A, show that there is another odd vertex u in A.

2.

Let x,y be two vertices in G with κ′(x,y)=2k for some integer k. Let X be the set of odd vertices in G and ∣X∣=2l. Show that there exists a disjoint pairing {(si​,ti​):1≤i≤l} of X, together with l edge-disjoint paths {Pi​:1≤i≤l}, where Pi​ joins si​ and ti​ for each i, such that after removing the edges ⋃i=1l​E(Pi​) from G, x and y are still 2k edge connected.

3.

A mouse eats its way through a 3×3×3 cube of cheese by eating all the 1×1×1 subcubes. If it starts at a corner subcube and always moves on to an adjacent subcube (sharing a face of area 1), can it do this and eat the center subcube last? Give a method or prove impossible. (Ignore gravity.)

4.

Recall that a graph is Class 1 if χ′(G)=Δ(G). Let G be a Class 1 regular graph of even degree. Prove that ∣E(G)∣ is even.

5.

The surface of a football is a planar graph with only 5-faces and 6-faces; Use graph theory to answer the following question (No credit without a proof).

(a) How many 5-faces of it?

(b) It is the smallest such planar graph with no adjacent 5-faces. How many vertices, faces and edges of it?

6.

Prove that a graph is k-colorable if every vertex lies in fewer than (2k​) odd cycles. (Hint: Use induction on ∣V(G)∣).

421 秋图论初步期末试卷 (吴河辉)

1.

For k∈N, prove that every graph G has a k-partite subgraph H with at least (1−1/k)∣E(G)∣ edges.

2.

Given a graph G, suppose that S⊆V(G) is covered by some matching. Prove or disprove each statement below:

(a)

S is covered by some maximum matching.

(b)

S is covered by every maximum matching.

3.

Let G be a 3-regular graph whose edge set has exactly one partition into three perfect matchings. Prove that G is Hamiltonian.

4.

Prove that the graph on the right is not 2-choosable.

5.

Prove or disprove: If G is a k-edge-connected graph with minimum degree greater than k, then some two vertices in G are joined by more than k-edge-disjoint paths.

6.

Let G be a planar graph with minimum degree 5. Let ei,j​ be the number of edges with endpoints of degrees i and j. Prove that e5,6​≥60 when e5,5​=0.
