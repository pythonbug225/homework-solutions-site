22024 秋季 (李志远)

1.

Let k be an algebraically closed field. Let H={f(x0​,…,xn​)=0}⊆Pkn​ be a hypersurface of degree d.

∘

Is H a connected scheme?

∘

How many irreducible components can H maximally have?

∘

Compute the dimension of Γ(H,OH​(1)).

2.

Consider the action of C∗ on AC3​∖{0} by λ⋅(x0​,x1​,x2​)=(λx0​,λx1​,λ2x2​).Set X=(AC3​∖{0})/C∗ to be the orbit space.

∘

Show that there is a natural scheme structure on X.

∘

Is X nonsingular? If not, find the singular point.

∘

Compute the class group of X.

3.

Let F be a quasi–coherent sheaf on a Noetherian scheme X.

∘

Show that the locus U={p∈X∣Fp​ is torsion free} is an open subset of X.

∘

Show that F is locally free if and only if Fp​ is a free Op​-module for all p∈X.

4.

Let C be the intersection of two quadric surfaces given by C:{x02​+x12​+x22​+x32​=x02​+x1​x2​+x3​x1​+x2​x3​=0}⊆PC3​.

∘

Compute the Picard group of C.

∘

Show there is only trivial morphism from C to the Fermat curve C0​:{x03​+x13​+x23​=0}⊆PC2​.

5.

Let k be a field. Let C be the image of Pk1​→Pkn​ under the map [x0​:x1​]↦[x0n​:x0n−1​x1​:x0n−2​x12​:⋯:x1n​].

∘

Show that C is smooth and is not a complete intersection if n>2, i.e. it is not an intersection of n−1 hypersurfaces.

∘

Show that C is not contained in any linear subspace of Pkn​.

∘

Show that if there exists another smooth curve C′⊆Pkn​ and C′≃Pk1​ is not contained in any linear subspace of Pkn​, then there exists a change of coordinates g∈GLn+1​(k) such that g(C)=C′.
