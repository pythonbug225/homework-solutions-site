126 春 (徐胜芝)

本次考试总时间约为 2.5 小时 (8:00–10:30)

一、

(30pts) Suppose that there is an exact sequence of smooth algebras 0→Jφ​Aψ​B→0.

(0)

Give the definition of the exponential and logarithm map to give the 6-term exact sequence of K-groups.

(1)

Prove that Kj​(B)=0 if B is trivial in multiplication: b1​b2​=0 for b1​, b2​∈B.

(2)

Prove that A2 as the closure of span(AA) is an ideal in A, and that A/A2 is trivially multiplicated. Prove that the inclusion map η:A2→A induces an isomorphism on K-groups.

二、

(20pts) For 0⩽t⩽1, define (ht​f)(z)=f(tz) for f∈C(D), where D={z∈C:∣z∣⩽1}.

(1)

Prove that (ht​:C(D)→C(D))0⩽t⩽1​ is a homotopy. Compute the K-groups Kj​(C(D)).

(2)

Recall that K0​(C(T))=Z[1], K1​(C(T))=Z[ζ], where ζ(z)=z. Prove that every continuous function f:D→D admits a fixed point.

三、

(20pts) Recall that the Morita invariance: for n∈Z+​, the map θ1​:x↦x⊕0⊕⋯⊕0 and θn​:x↦0⊕⋯⊕0⊕x from a smooth algebra B to the matrix algebra B(n) induce the same isomorphism on K-groups.

(1)

Let B be a closed ideal of a unital smooth algebra D. Prove that the automorphism adb​:x↦bxb−1 for b∈D× induces the identical map on K-groups.

(2)

Let φ,ψ:A→B be two morphisms between smooth algebras such that ψ(x)=uφ(x)v and φ(x)=vψ(x)u for some {u,v}⊆D with uvu=u and vuv=v. Prove that φ and ψ induce the same morphism on K-groups.

四、

(30pts) Let A be a C∗-algebra and HA​ be the canonical Hilbert A-module.

(1)

Prove that KA​(HA​)=A⊗K(l2) and the morphism γ:A→A⊗K(l2),a↦a⊗e induces the same isomorphism on K-groups for any projection e on l2 of rank 1.

(2)

Prove that any adjointable isometry operator V:X→HA​ between Hilbert A-modules induces a morphism adV​:T↦VTV∗ from KA​(X) to KA​(HA​) which induces a morphism on K-groups, denoted by γX​, independent on V.

(3)

Give the definition of (adjointable) Fredholm operators T:X→Y between Hilbert A-modules and construct its index if X⊕Y⊕Z≅HA​ for some Hilbert A-module Z.
