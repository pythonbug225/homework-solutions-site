Problem 1.

Let A be an amenable C∗-algebra. Prove that the unitization A is also an amenable C∗-algebra.

Problem 2.

Let ϕ:A→B be a completely positive linear map, where A and B are C∗-algebras. Show that ϕ(a)∗ϕ(a)≤∥ϕ∥ϕ(a∗a)for every a∈A.
